# 40 Love — investor brief

**A tennis club for players forty and over.** Courts, open hits, coaching, monthly social gatherings, and good company nearby. Friendships first; should something more grow from it, all the better.

*Founded by Joyce and her mom, for the love of connecting with others on the court.*

---

## The problem

Tennis is booming, and the people driving it are increasingly adults who are coming back to the sport or picking it up later in life. But finding a regular game is still solved by group texts, club bulletin boards, and luck. Existing apps are built for the young and the competitive: ladder rankings, swiping, and match-making that feels like dating. A 52-year-old 3.5 who wants a friendly Tuesday doubles game and a glass of wine afterwards has nowhere to go.

## The opportunity

- **27.3 million** Americans played tennis in 2025, a record, up **54% since 2019** and the sixth straight year of growth. (USTA, 2026 U.S. Tennis Participation Report, Feb 18 2026)
- **1.7 million** players returned to tennis in 2025 after a break of a year or more, and the USTA specifically cites **older adults** among the segments broadening the sport.
- More than **25 million** non-players say they are "very interested" in playing — a latent pool that is disproportionately adults who want an easy, social on-ramp.
- Adults 40+ have the discretionary time, income, and loyalty that makes a paid community sustainable; they book lessons, buy tickets, and show up on Friday.

## The product

A phone-first app with five pillars. The working demo in this repository is fully interactive.

| Pillar      | What it does                                                                                   |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Map**     | GPS map of nearby courts with live open-hit counts and a draggable sheet, like a ride-share app |
| **Hits**    | Browse open invitations (no swiping), see skill/NTRP and rankings, join, or post your own      |
| **Socials** | The signature *Second Friday* pub social every month, plus club trips (Indian Wells first)     |
| **Lessons** | Marketplace of vetted 40+-friendly coaches with reservations                                   |
| **Chat**    | Citywide clubhouse, auto-created group chats per hit and event, direct messages               |

**What makes it different:** it is a *club*, not a marketplace. Membership is 40+ only, tone is warm and grown-up, and the calendar has a heartbeat (Second Friday) that turns app users into a real community.

## Business model

| Stream                          | Mechanics                                                            | Status        |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| **Coach Pro subscription**      | $29/month for featured placement + Pro Partner badge; coach keeps 100% | Built in demo |
| **Coach partner revenue share** | 15% per booking, no monthly fee                                        | Built in demo |
| **Member premium tier**         | Priority RSVP, saved courts, guest passes (~$8–12/month)               | Roadmap       |
| **Events & trips**              | Ticketing margin on socials; packaged tournament trips                 | Roadmap       |
| **Local partners**              | Pubs, clubs, and racket shops as venue and sponsor partners            | Roadmap       |

Coaches are the wedge: they bring their existing students, and every lesson booked in-app seeds the social graph.

## Go-to-market

1. **Portland first.** Launch with 5–8 public courts, a founding cohort of ~200 members, and the first Second Friday at a partner pub. The founders already host it.
2. **Coaches as ambassadors.** Recruit 10 local coaches on the partner plan; each brings 15–30 active students.
3. **City playbook.** Package the launch (courts, coaches, pub, first social) and repeat in Seattle, Sacramento, Denver, and Phoenix — cities with strong adult tennis and outdoor seasons.

## Traction & roadmap

- **Now:** complete, tested front-end base (this repo) — every screen and flow works, persistence included.
- **Next 90 days:** backend (accounts, 40+ verification, hits, chat), Stripe payments, first 3 coaches, first live Second Friday with in-app RSVP.
- **6 months:** App Store / Google Play, 1,000 Portland members, coach subscription revenue.
- **12 months:** second city, member premium tier, first Indian Wells trip.
